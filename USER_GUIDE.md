# User Guide

Welcome. This app is your personal casino, pointed at the habits you actually want to do. It takes about five minutes to learn and then gets out of your way. No jargon below — just how it works and why it works.

---

## 1. Why this works (the 60-second version)

Your brain isn't motivated by **big** rewards. It's motivated by **maybe**.

A guaranteed reward goes stale. An uncertain reward produces one of the biggest natural dopamine spikes ever measured — just shy of cocaine. That's why slot machines eat casinos' rent. That's why your phone is so addictive. That's why doomscrolling the news for the next outrage hit keeps you up past 1am.

This app borrows the exact mechanic and points it somewhere useful: your habits.

Three ideas do all the work:

- **Maybe > big.** Uncertainty beats magnitude. Every habit earns a random-colored clip, not a known reward.
- **Near-miss is a feature.** When the wheel slides *just past* the big prize, your dopamine spikes anyway. The app leans into that on purpose.
- **Just a little bit more.** Right after you did 15 burpees, 6 more feels like a steal. The bonus timer exploits that window.

If you can't stay consistent, the problem isn't willpower. Your habits are just less fun than your phone. Let's fix that.

## 2. Your three pieces

Everything is built on three things.

### The wheel

Your reward engine. It has five slots:

- **T1 (40%)** — small, common reward
- **T2 (30%)** — medium reward
- **T3 (20%)** — big, rare reward
- **BONUS (8%)** — you get your best available reward for free, then you spin the bonus wheel
- **JACKPOT (2%)** — instant T3 plus a free bonus spin, no matter what you've cashed in

The wheel tells you what you won. Which specific reward you take from that tier is your call — you pick from your own menu.

### The clips (your paperclip bag)

Your bag starts with **60 colored paperclips** (six colors, ten each) plus **one gold clip**. Every time you finish a habit, you draw one random clip from the bag and it goes into your **hand**. The bag shrinks as you draw; when it empties, it refills automatically.

**Color matching** is how you unlock better rewards. Same color = unlock higher tiers.

**Gold clip** is the whole addictive hook. Rare. Glows. Changes the sound. When you draw one, you get an instant Tier 3 reward — you don't even spin.

### The jar

Every clip you earn drops **$1** into a virtual jar. You set three milestones: **Mini**, **Mid**, **Moonshot**. The jar fills toward Moonshot — Mini and Mid are tick marks you cross on the way. When you hit one, you get a celebration + an "unlocked" badge, and the app asks whether you've actually claimed the real-world thing yet.

## 3. The core loop

Every time you finish a habit:

1. **DO** the habit (15 burpees, 20 min cleaning, 2,500 steps, whatever).
2. **GRAB A CLIP** — tap the habit in the app. It draws one random clip from the bag into your hand.
3. **CASH IN** (optional) — match same-color clips to unlock higher tiers.
4. **SPIN** — tap Spin. Claim your reward.

That's it. Five taps, under a minute, done.

---

## 4. Cashing in (the gating rules)

Before you spin, you decide whether to cash in clips from your hand. Cashed-in clips get **returned to the bag** — you don't lose them permanently.

| You cash in | What pays out |
|---|---|
| **Nothing** | Only Tier 1 pays out. If the wheel lands on T2 or T3, it's a **near-miss** — no reward, close-call animation. |
| **2 matching** (same color) | T1 and T2 pay out. T3 is still a near-miss. |
| **3 matching** (same color) | All tiers pay out normally. |
| **1 gold clip** | **Instant Tier 3. No spin.** You pick a T3 reward right away. |

Even on losing spins, the wheel visually drifts past a locked tier before settling. That's intentional — your dopamine spikes anyway, and that's the whole point.

## 5. The bonus wheel

If the main wheel lands on **BONUS**, two things happen:

1. You auto-collect your best-available reward (whatever tier you had unlocked) — for free, no picking needed.
2. A second wheel appears with five slots.

| Slot | Odds | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **75%** | 35% | Do 75% of a habit in 10 minutes → earn 1 clip |
| **50%** | 25% | Do 50% of a habit in 10 minutes → earn 1 clip |
| **25%** | 15% | Do 25% of a habit in 10 minutes → earn 1 clip |
| **FREE** | 10% | Free clip, no habit required |
| **EXTRA** | 15% | Re-spin the bonus wheel — chain streaks |

**The 10-minute rule:** as soon as the bonus wheel stops, a 10-minute timer starts. A sticky banner shows the countdown from every screen. Finish the discounted habit in time → earn the clip. Miss the window → no clip, no streak. Keep whatever you already got from the original spin.

The trick is the "just a little bit more" principle. You *just did* 15 burpees. What's 6 more? It feels cheap, because your brain is comparing it to what you did 30 seconds ago. That's how one session becomes three clips. That's how chains happen.

## 6. The jar (Mini / Mid / Moonshot)

The jar is your long-game prize ladder. $1 per clip, automatic.

- **Mini** — something small. A new book. A specialty coffee. Draw it low.
- **Mid** — medium splurge. A nice dinner out. A fun class.
- **Moonshot** — the thing you actually want. Hawaii upgrade. Spa weekend. Concert. Woodworking class. Something experiential that makes the whole grind feel worth it.

When you cross a Mini or Mid threshold, you get an unlock animation and a "claim" button. Claiming is your cue that you actually did/bought the thing in the real world. The jar total **keeps climbing** through Mini and Mid — you're building toward Moonshot the whole time.

When you cross Moonshot and claim it, the jar resets to $0 and the app asks if you want to reuse the same milestones or pick new ones. Then you go again.

---

## 7. Streaks

Three streaks run in the background:

- **Daily streak** — consecutive days with at least one clip earned. Miss a day and it resets.
- **Hygiene bundle streak** — consecutive days you checked off all four hygiene sub-items (shower, brush teeth, wash face, in bed by 1am) before your cutoff. The clip is awarded retroactively the next time you open the app.
- **Bonus chain** — your current unbroken run of bonus-wheel completions in a single session. Miss a 10-minute timer → chain ends.

Streaks are there to catch your eye, not to punish you. Missing a day is fine. The casino doesn't care. Tomorrow is a new session.

## 8. The 3 reward rules

Your rewards aren't arbitrary. Pick ones that obey **all three** of these rules — this is the whole load-bearing part of the system. Quoted from the PDF:

### #1 — Highly Addicting

**Thinking about it should give you a rush. If it doesn't spike your dopamine on its own, it won't power the system.**

### #2 — Time or Money Wasting

**Pick something you regret: doomscrolling, phone games, takeout. You want the system to REDUCE this.**

### #3 — The Naked Rule

**Never do this reward 'naked' again — only through the system. Otherwise the casino is irrelevant.**

If you doomscroll without winning a doomscroll, the system has no teeth. The Naked Rule is what makes everything else work. Commit to it at onboarding. The app will show you the three rules whenever you edit your reward menu as a reminder.

## 9. Mobile tips

### Add to Home Screen

On iPhone:

1. Open the site in **Safari** (not Chrome — Chrome can't install PWAs on iOS).
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with arrow).
3. Scroll down, tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Launch it from your home screen icon — it runs full-screen, no browser chrome.

Why it matters:

- **Offline.** Once installed, the app works with no network. The whole casino fits in your service worker cache.
- **Durability.** Installing the PWA asks iOS to keep your data around longer. Your bag, hand, jar, streaks, history — all of it lives in your phone's local database. Without the PWA install, iOS Safari may evict that database if you don't visit for a few weeks.
- **Lock-screen reachable.** One tap to log a habit. Zero friction. Pro tip: keep a stopwatch pinned to your lock screen too.

### Export regularly

Cloud sync is on the roadmap (sign in once, the same jar follows you to any device in real time). Until it ships — and as a belt-and-suspenders backup even after it does — **export your data to a JSON file** every week or two (Settings → Export). If your phone dies, gets wiped, or iOS evicts the database, import restores everything exactly.

## 10. FAQ

**What happens if I miss the 10-minute bonus timer?**
No clip. Streak ends. You keep whatever reward you earned from the original main-wheel spin. The casino doesn't care. Move on.

**What's the difference between BONUS and JACKPOT?**
- **BONUS** (8%) = auto-collect your currently-available reward (T1 if you cashed in nothing, T2 if you had 2-match, T3 if you had 3-match), THEN spin the bonus wheel.
- **JACKPOT** (2%) = instant T3 **regardless of what you cashed in**, PLUS a free bonus wheel spin. You don't need to have unlocked T3 — Jackpot bypasses the gating rules entirely.

**Can I edit my rewards?**
Yes. Rewards → T1 / T2 / T3 tabs → add, edit, archive, done. Pre-populated with a starter set you can keep, tweak, or wipe. The three reward rules are displayed next to the editor so you remember to vet new entries.

**Can I edit my habits?**
Yes. Habits → edit, archive, add new. Five are seeded by default (Walk, Workout, Cleaning, Focused Work, Hygiene bundle). The Walk habit takes step counts and gives you one clip per 2,500 steps — enter 10,000 steps and get 4 clips at once.

**Can I change the wheel probabilities?**
Yes, Settings → Wheel split. The defaults (40/30/20/8/2) match the PDF. If you tune them, the editor auto-normalizes so they sum to 100%.

**Can I change the bag composition?**
Yes, Settings → Bag composition. Defaults are 10 of each of 6 colors + 1 gold = 61 total. More gold = faster instant-T3 wins (and faster dopamine desensitization — be careful).

**What if I accidentally cash in the wrong clips?**
The app returns cashed-in clips to the bag, not the trash — so you haven't "lost" them. But the effect on the current spin is locked in once you confirm. Hit Spin with intent.

**What if I do the habit before I have the app open?**
Fine. The order is DO → GRAB → CASH → SPIN. Doing the habit is step one regardless. Open the app after, tap the habit, spin.

**How do I reset everything?**
Settings → Reset All. Triple-confirmation gate. Nukes your bag, hand, jar, streaks, and history — not recoverable unless you exported first.

**Why one gold clip instead of three?**
Three gold in 63 clips makes gold events too frequent (~3/week at normal pace). One gold in 61 clips lands around once a week — rare enough to stay special. You can tune this in Settings → Bag composition.

**Do my rewards get "used up" after I claim one?**
No. The reward menu is an inspiration list. When the wheel says "Tier 2," you pick any T2 reward you want — none are consumed. Your history log records which ones you actually picked so you can see patterns over time.

**The app says there's a new version — do I have to update?**
Yes, tap Reload. New versions sometimes include migrations; skipping them can leave you on an older schema. The update prompt is there so you're in control of *when* it lands (not mid-spin).

**Can I run multiple jars (one per habit stack)?**
Not in v1. The schema is ready for it — every internal piece already carries a jar ID — but the UI is single-jar for now. Multi-jar (fitness / brain / side-hustle, per the PDF) is on the v1.1 list.

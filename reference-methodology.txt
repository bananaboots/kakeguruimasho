  THE PSYCHOLOGY BEHIND IT

  The 'just a little bit more' principle.
  Your brain evaluates effort relative to what you JUST did.
  Right after 15 burpees, 6 more feels like a steal — even
  though you'd never do 21 back-to-back without the system.
  Same trick slot machines use to pull 'one more spin' out of
  gamblers.
